Settings & help

Steps/min is estimated from belt speed. If it feels off, count your steps for 30 s, double it and press Calibrate. If your machine reports real cadence, that is used instead. Matching works on tempo, not beat position: the beat will hold your pace but may land between footfalls; the Click track helps you lock in.
